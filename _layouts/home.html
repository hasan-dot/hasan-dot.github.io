01 / What I work on

queries · capacity · reliability

I spend a lot of time with MySQL and Vitess. Sometimes a query is slow. Other times, it's fast but we're running it far too often. Query plans, Datadog graphs, flame graphs, and logs help me figure out what's going on.

At GitHub's scale, a query or schema change can affect much more than the feature that introduced it. I work across teams to understand that impact, reduce unnecessary database work, and keep capacity ahead of demand. All of that has to happen while GitHub keeps serving developers around the world. That also means helping teams meet their availability SLOs, improving monitoring so we catch problems early, and responding to incidents when things go wrong.
